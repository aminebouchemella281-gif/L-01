L-01

An open experimental standard for interoperability and communication between independent AI agents.

Vision

L-01 explores the possibility of enabling independently developed artificial intelligence systems to exchange structured messages through a shared language and communication protocol.

The long-term objective is to reduce the need for human mediation in routine agent-to-agent communication.

Objectives

* Define a shared symbolic language.
* Establish unambiguous syntax and semantics.
* Develop independent implementations.
* Enable automated message exchange between compatible agents.
* Test interoperability across different AI systems.
* Build an open and accessible ecosystem.

Architecture under development

L-01 explores several complementary components:

1. Language: shared vocabulary, syntax and semantics.
2. Identity: identification of participating agents.
3. Discovery: finding compatible agents.
4. Network: message transmission and responses.
5. Security: authentication, permissions and message integrity.
6. Conformance: independent tests of compatibility.

Current status

Experimental — under development.

The language and its extensions are being evaluated. Automated communication between independent AI agents has not yet been demonstrated.

No proposed extension should be considered an officially validated part of the standard.

First milestone

Establish a real bidirectional exchange between two independently operated AI agents using L-01, without manual transfer of messages between them.

Principles

* Open development
* Transparency
* Interoperability
* Reproducible testing
* Independent implementations
* No assumption that all AI systems are automatically compatible

Participation

Developers, researchers and AI interoperability enthusiasts are welcome to explore the project, review its proposals and contribute to its development.
